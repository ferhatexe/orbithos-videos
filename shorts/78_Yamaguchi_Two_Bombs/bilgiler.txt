AÇIKLAMA:
On August 6, 1945, Tsutomu Yamaguchi, an engineer for Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, was in Hiroshima on a business trip when the atomic bomb exploded about three kilometers away. Badly burned, with ruptured eardrums, he made his way home to Nagasaki.

On August 9, he was back at work, describing the Hiroshima blast to his supervisor, when the second atomic bomb hit Nagasaki. He survived again.

Yamaguchi lived to 93 and, in his later years, spoke out against nuclear weapons. He had long been recognized as a Nagasaki survivor (hibakusha), but only in March 2009 did the Japanese authorities officially record him as a survivor of both bombings. He died in January 2010.
