AÇIKLAMA:
The Romans named the seven days of the week after the seven "wandering lights" in the sky: the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn. When Germanic peoples adopted the week, they swapped in their own gods.

Mars, god of war, became Tiw (Týr), the one-handed god of war and law: Tiw's day, Tuesday. Mercury became Woden — the Norse Odin: Wednesday. Jupiter the thunderer became Thor: Thursday. And Venus became Frigg, Odin's wife: Friday. Saturday simply kept the Roman Saturn.

In Spanish, the Roman gods never left: martes (Mars), miércoles (Mercury), jueves (Jupiter), viernes (Venus).
