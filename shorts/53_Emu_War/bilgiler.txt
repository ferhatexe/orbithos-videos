AÇIKLAMA:
In late 1932, about 20,000 emus invaded the wheat farms of Western Australia. Many of the farmers were World War I veterans, and they asked the army for help. The army sent a major, two soldiers, two Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds of ammunition.

It did not go to plan. The emus scattered into small groups, a machine gun jammed, and when the soldiers mounted a gun on a truck, the birds simply outran it — an emu can sprint at about 50 km/h. Major Meredith later said the emus could face machine guns "with the invulnerability of tanks."

After nearly ten thousand rounds, the army pulled out. Farmers asked for military help again in 1934, 1943 and 1948. Each time, the answer was no. The emus won.
