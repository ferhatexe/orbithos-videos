Pandora Never Opened a Box — It Was a 500-Year Mistake 📦
AÇIKLAMA:
In Greek myth, Zeus ordered the gods to make the first woman, Pandora. Each god gave her a gift, and she was given a sealed container holding sickness, toil and every evil of the world. When she lifted the lid, everything escaped — except one thing: Hope.

But the poet Hesiod, around 700 BC, never wrote "box." He wrote "pithos" — a huge clay storage jar, often as tall as a person, used for wine, oil and grain. The philosopher Diogenes supposedly lived in one.

Then, in 1508, the Renaissance scholar Erasmus retold the story in Latin and wrote "pyxis" — a box. The mistake stuck for five hundred years, in paintings, books and the phrase we still use today.
